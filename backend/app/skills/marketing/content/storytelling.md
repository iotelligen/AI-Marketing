# Storytelling Skill

## Objective
Communicate industrial problems through relatable stories.

## Recommended Structure
1. Introduce a realistic industrial situation.
2. Identify the operational problem.
3. Explain its possible consequences.
4. Introduce a practical technical solution.
5. Describe the expected benefit without inventing results.
6. Finish with a call to action.

Use realistic factory scenarios relevant to Bangladesh.

Do not present hypothetical scenarios as real customer case studies.
