# Technical Content Skill

## Objective
Ensure technical content is accurate and understandable.

## Guidelines
- Explain technologies using correct terminology.
- Distinguish PLC control from IoT monitoring.
- Explain protocols such as Modbus RTU, Modbus TCP and MQTT correctly.
- Do not invent product specifications.
- Do not invent customer deployments or performance results.
- Use practical industrial examples.
- Explain technical concepts in business-friendly language.

## Evidence
Use retrieved knowledge for IoTelliGen-specific claims.
Clearly distinguish general technical explanations from verified company facts.
If information is unavailable, do not fabricate it.
