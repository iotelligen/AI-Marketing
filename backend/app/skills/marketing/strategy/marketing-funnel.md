# Marketing Funnel Skill

## Objective
Align content with the audience's position in the buying journey.

## Awareness
Goal: Make the audience recognize an industrial problem.

Content examples:
- Common factory inefficiencies
- Energy waste
- Unexpected machine downtime
- Production visibility problems

## Consideration
Goal: Help the audience understand possible solutions.

Content examples:
- PLC vs Industrial IoT
- Predictive maintenance
- Energy monitoring
- Industrial gateways
- Technical demonstrations

## Conversion
Goal: Encourage business enquiries.

Content examples:
- Product demonstrations
- Solution explanations
- Consultation offers
- Pilot project discussions

## Guidelines
Use the objective supplied by the user.
Do not assume every post should directly sell a product.
