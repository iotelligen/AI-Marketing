# Instagram Content Skill

## Objective
Create visual-first content that communicates industrial technology
and business value.

## Writing Guidelines
- Prioritize short, engaging captions.
- Use a strong opening line.
- Focus on one key message per post.
- Suggest visual concepts where appropriate.
- Use concise explanations.
- Include a relevant call to action.
- Use 5–10 relevant hashtags.

Content should work alongside images, carousels or short videos.
