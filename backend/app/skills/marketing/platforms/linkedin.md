# LinkedIn Content Skill

## Objective
Create professional B2B content for industrial decision-makers.

## Audience
Factory owners, CEOs, operations managers, production managers,
maintenance engineers and industrial decision-makers in Bangladesh.

## Writing Guidelines
- Start with a strong business or technical hook.
- Focus on industrial problems and practical solutions.
- Explain business impact, not just technology.
- Use short paragraphs and readable formatting.
- Include a relevant question or call to action.
- Use 3–6 relevant hashtags.

## Content Structure
1. Hook
2. Problem
3. Technical explanation or solution
4. Business impact
5. Call to action

Avoid generic motivational content and exaggerated claims.
