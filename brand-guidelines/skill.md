# IoTelliGen Brand Guidelines

## Company
IoTelliGen develops Industrial IoT solutions for industrial
automation, monitoring and operational efficiency.

## Vision
Increase the efficiency of industry in Bangladesh.

## Mission
- Promote Industry 4.0 adoption.
- Introduce practical industrial automation.
- Reduce maintenance costs.
- Reduce power consumption.

## Target Audience
- Factory owners
- Factory directors
- Production managers
- Maintenance engineers
- Electrical engineers
- Industrial decision-makers

## Geographic Focus
Bangladesh.

## Brand Positioning
IoTelliGen provides practical Industrial IoT solutions
that help factories understand, monitor and improve
their operations.

## Communication Style
- Professional and technically credible.
- Clear and practical.
- Avoid exaggerated marketing claims.
- Explain technical concepts in business language.
- Focus on measurable industrial problems.

## Restrictions
- Never invent customers, projects or achievements.
- Never invent product specifications.
- Do not claim guaranteed cost savings.
- Do not describe IoT as a replacement for PLC control.
- Use knowledge-base information for company-specific claims.
